A part of a page under a heading, such as a few of the projects on the portfolio's top page.

`title` is the heading, an `h2`. `more` is a link at the end of the heading's line to the page that holds the whole of what the section shows a part of ("All 8 projects"); leave it out where there is no such page. The children go under the heading, `space-10` apart from one another.

In a `ProfilePage` a section stands `space-24` from what is above it, more than anything inside it is apart, so that the sections of the top page read as the lists they are.

- Do: put under it a list set for a section (`titleAs="h3"` on a `ProjectGrid`, a `TalkFeature` or a `TalkList`), a `WritingList`, a brief `CareerList` or a `Prose`.
- Don't: use it inside a post, whose headings are its Markdown's, or nest one in another.
