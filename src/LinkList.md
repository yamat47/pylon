A short row of links in the small text style, such as a site's navigation or the links at the foot of a page.

`links` is the list, in the order to show, each with its `label` and `href`. The row wraps when the page is narrow. It is a plain list: put it in a `nav` where it is the way around the site, as `SiteHeader` does.

- Do: keep labels to a word or two.
- Don't: use it for a list a reader works through, such as posts; that is an `IndexList`.
