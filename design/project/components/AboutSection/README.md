One thing the about page tells of the person: their work, open source, American football, games. A heading, the text, photographs where there are some, and links to read more.

`title` is the heading, an `h2`. The children are the text, as paragraphs (`p`), set as a `Prose` sets them, in the page's body text. `photos` are photographs, 4:3, side by side under the text and one under another on a narrow window. `links` are a row of where to read more: a page of the site, a post. A link to something in another language takes that language as its `lang`.

In a `ProfilePage` the sections stand `space-14` apart.

- Do: tell one thing in each section, in a few sentences, as the person would say it.
- Don't: use it for a list (the projects, the talks, the career have their own), or put a photograph in it for decoration.
