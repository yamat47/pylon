The person's projects as a grid of screenshots, each with its name, its years, a line on what it is and its links.

`projects` is the list, in the order to show. A project has a `name`, the year or month it began (`start`) and, where it has, ended (`end`), each as `YYYY` or `YYYY-MM`: shown as `2021 –` while it goes on and as `2021` when it began and ended that year. `description` is a sentence or two in the muted ink, and `links` a row of where it is (its code, its package, a demo). `screenshot` is a 16:10 picture of it; left out, its place is tinted.

The grid fills its column with as many columns of at least 18rem as fit, and falls into one on a narrow window. `titleAs` is the element of each name: `h2` (the default) where the grid is the page's content under its `h1`, `h3` in a `Section`.

- Do: write the description as what the project is, and what came of it, in a sentence a reader outside the field can follow.
- Don't: add tags, stars or a list of technologies to a project. A project is a picture, a name, its years, a line and its links.
