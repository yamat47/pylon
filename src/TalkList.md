Talks led by their dates: the year small over a large day, then the title, the event and the links.

`talks` is the list, newest first. A talk is what a `TalkFeature` takes: its `title`, in its own `lang` where that is not the page's, the `date` it was given as `YYYY-MM-DD`, the `event`, and its `links`. The year and the day share a narrow column, so the titles line up beside them; a long title wraps under itself, and breaks between phrases where the page says it is Japanese.

`titleAs` is the element of each title: `h2` (the default) on the page of talks, under its `h1`, and `h3` in a `Section`.

- Do: follow a `TalkFeature` of the newest talk with the rest, here.
- Don't: give a talk a picture in this list. Only the newest has its slide.
