The paragraph that opens the portfolio's top page and its about page: who the person is, a step larger than the text.

The children are the paragraph, in `text-lead`, or in `text-lead-latin` when `lang` is `en`: the same size on a shorter line, as English needs. `more` is a small link under it to where the person says more ("More about me"), for the top page; the about page has none.

- Do: keep it to one paragraph of a few sentences, written as the person would say them.
- Don't: use it for a post's first paragraph, or for a slogan: it is the introduction, once on a page.
