What the person has written, as titles over their dates: no summary, and no word of where each was published.

`articles` is the list, newest first. An article is what a `PostIndex` takes, a `title` that links to `href` and the `date` it is dated as `YYYY-MM-DD`, with the title's `lang` where that is not the page's: a Japanese post keeps its Japanese title on an English page.

As it is, the list dates each article in full (`2025.10.14`), for a few of them in a `Section`. Set `grouped` on the page of writing: the articles go under their years, each an `h2` in the style for numerals, and each article is dated by its month and day (`10.14`). Either way a title is an `h3`.
