A picture in one of the shapes the look gives pictures, cropped to fit it, with the one rounding.

`image` is its address (`src`) and what it shows (`alt`), said to a reader who cannot see it. `ratio` is its shape, width to height: `1:1` for the photo of the person, `16:10` for a project's screenshot, `16:9` for a talk's first slide, `3:2` and `4:3` for the about page's photographs. It is as wide as what it is in, and as tall as its shape makes it.

A picture that is not there yet (leave `image` out) or fails to load leaves its place tinted, so the page keeps its shape. `ProfileAside`, `ProjectGrid`, `TalkFeature` and `AboutSection` set their pictures with it; a page uses it by itself for the photograph that opens the about page.

- Do: give every picture an `alt` that says what is in it.
- Don't: use it for decoration. A picture here is a photograph, a screenshot or a slide.
