The bottom of every page of both sites: a band of the dark theme across the window, with the name, the links every page carries and the copyright.

`name` links to `href`, the top page: the site's name on the blog, the person's on the portfolio. `description` is one line under it. `groups` are the links, each under its `label` and one under another: the site's pages, where else the person is, and on the portfolio the language. Mark the page the reader is on, and the language they read in, `current`: those are underlined and the others are not. Under a rule comes the copyright: `©`, the `year` and the `holder`.

The band is the one band of color the look allows. It is `color-surface-footer` in the page's theme, and what is on it is drawn in the dark theme's colors in either theme. Its content is `width-page` wide: the name where a `ProfileAside` stands, the groups where the content stands. On a narrow window the groups move under the name.

- Do: give every page of a site the same footer, but for the links marked `current`.
- Don't: put text to read in it, or a link a reader needs to get through a page: the footer is where a reader ends up, not where they start.
